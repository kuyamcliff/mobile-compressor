LGPL compliance (FFmpeg, LAME, FriBidi)
---------------------------------------
* The LGPL components are contained in the separate shared libraries
  libavcodec.so, libavformat.so, libavfilter.so, libavutil.so, libswscale.so
  and libswresample.so in the APK's lib/arm64-v8a directory. The app's own
  code (libvcengine.so and the Kotlin code) only uses their public API, so the
  libraries can be replaced by a modified build of the same ABI.
* Complete corresponding source: FFmpeg 9.0.2 from
  https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-9.0.2.tar.xz plus the two local patches in
  native/third_party/patches/ (ffmpeg-0001-libopenh264enc-constant-qp.patch,
  ffmpeg-0002-vf_subtitles-fallback-font.patch), LAME 3.100 and FriBidi
  1.0.17 from the URLs in versions.env. The exact build script is
  native/third_party/build_deps.sh in the app's source repository. Anyone
  distributing this APK must make that corresponding source available to its
  recipients (LGPL-2.1 section 6).

Patent notice
-------------
Video codecs such as H.264, HEVC and AV1 may be covered by patents in some
jurisdictions. OpenH264 is compiled from source here, so Cisco's binary patent
license for its prebuilt OpenH264 does not apply. Distributors are responsible
for any patent licensing their distribution requires. Hardware encoders and
decoders are provided by the device through Android's MediaCodec API.
